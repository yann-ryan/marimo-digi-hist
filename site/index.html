Text analysis labs

Hands-on tasters in computational text analysis for historians: counting words, phrases and collocations, comparing groups, and word embeddings. Each lab runs entirely in your browser.

The Old Bailey Proceedings

1674–1913 · London’s central criminal court · English

240 years of trials: from watchmen to policemen, from guineas to sovereigns.

The Royal Society Corpus

1665–1920 · Philosophical Transactions and Proceedings · English

250 years of science writing: from curious and ingenious to results, from phlogiston to oxygen.

The Dutch parliamentary proceedings

1814–2012 · Handelingen der Staten-Generaal · Dutch

Two centuries of debate: from zoo to zo, from Indië to Europa, from onderdanen to burgers.

How to start

  1. Use Chrome on a laptop. Nothing to install, no account needed.
  2. Open a lab and wait about 30 seconds while it loads.
  3. Click the ▶ “run all” button (bottom right) once.
  4. Your changes are not saved when you reload: use the menu to download your notebook if you want to keep it.
